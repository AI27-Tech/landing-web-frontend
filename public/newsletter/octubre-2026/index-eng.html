MOST STOLEN TRANSPORT
BY VEHICLE TYPE

During the month analyzed, private vehicles recorded 335 theft attempts per 10,000 units, a 49% decrease compared to September. In contrast, the Thorton and Trailer categories showed an increase in their incidence indicators, rising from 5 to 16 and from 4 to 8 attempts per 10,000 units, respectively, which represents increases of 220% and 100%.

In addition, pickup trucks and/or vehicles up to 5 tons also showed an increase, going from 1 to 8 attempts per 10,000 units. Likewise, the Rabón truck category recorded 28 attempts per 10,000 units, while the 53-ft box trailer reported an indicator of 6,667, making it the category with the highest incidence reported during October. Overall, there is an increase in exposure across several cargo transport categories, in contrast to the decrease recorded for private vehicles.

HOTSPOT MAPS

SESNSP CARGO THEFT · SEPTEMBER 2026
AI27 CARGO THEFT · SEPTEMBER 2026

According to the SESNSP, Estado de México and Puebla remain the main cargo theft hotspots nationwide in September 2026, concentrating 43% and 29% of reported cases respectively, out of a total of 414 registered incidents. They are followed by Michoacán with 13%, while San Luis Potosí accounts for 6% and Tabasco for 3%, keeping these states among the areas of highest incidence for the period. Compared with August (392 thefts), September shows a 5.6% increase in total cases, rising from 392 to 414 incidents and maintaining the upward trend seen the previous month. Although Estado de México’s share fell from 50% to 43%, Puebla’s concentration rose from 24% to 29%, showing a territorial redistribution of events, with risk remaining concentrated in both states, which together account for 72% of national incidents.

In AI27’s records for September 2026, the risk distribution shows a greater concentration in the logistics corridors of central Mexico and the Bajío. Puebla leads with 33%, followed by Estado de México with 20%, while Guanajuato accounts for 12% of events. Jalisco and Veracruz account for 8% each, followed by Querétaro with 6% and San Luis Potosí with 4%. Compared with August, the rise in Puebla’s share stands out, going from 13% to 33% and becoming the state with the highest incidence in internal records, while Estado de México showed a slight decrease from 21% to 20%. Likewise, Guanajuato increased its share from 7% to 12%, while Jalisco fell from 15% to 8%. The distribution recorded during the period reflects a greater concentration of events in Puebla, Estado de México and Guanajuato, which together account for 65% of internal incidence, evidencing significant exposure in the main logistics corridors of central Mexico and the Bajío.

GENERAL RECOMMENDATIONS

To improve cargo transportation security, it is recommended to:

Use of predictive technology: employ advanced systems that analyze real-time information to anticipate potential threats, providing early warnings and allowing companies to make preventive decisions about the routes to take.

Operator training: train drivers in defensive driving techniques, recognizing danger signs, and knowing how to act in risky situations to protect both the cargo and their well-being.

Monitoring and immediate response: use constant monitoring systems to maintain visibility over the status of the units, allowing for a quick response to any incident and coordination with security forces.

Integration of digital security tools: incorporate technological solutions that provide an additional layer of security in the transportation process, such as tracking systems and advanced monitoring for efficient risk management.

Investment in security infrastructure: invest in advanced technology such as surveillance cameras, load sensors, and GPS systems to strengthen the security infrastructure of transportation units and minimize the impact of any theft attempts.

At AI27, we specialize in providing technological solutions that protect companies in their logistics operations. Through advanced monitoring tools and predictive analysis, we work to mitigate risks on routes and enhance the security of goods in transit. Our comprehensive approach combines technological innovation with rapid response services, contributing to the safety of the logistics industry and ensuring the protection of both operators and cargo.
